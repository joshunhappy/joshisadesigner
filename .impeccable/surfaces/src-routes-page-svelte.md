## Direction contract

THESIS: Warp is the design, weft is the build; the cloth exists only where both cross. It refuses the monochrome name + photo + card-grid portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Indigo warp field (#1E2A78 family) owns at least half of home. Weft colours are cochineal, marigold and jade. Undyed cotton is the reading surface for long case-study text only. Projects are brocade bands: horizontal woven strips whose pick density encodes rank. Gallery-only projects are bare warp: loose vertical threads labelled "Screens only". Controls are woven tabs; the active state is a tightly beaten band. Grid cells re-count per viewport and never stretch (raise from Hoffmann). Rank and focus use one integer density scale in one stylesheet (raise from cracktro). Absence is drawn deliberately (raise from seven-segment). One continuous marigold weft thread runs portrait → bands → contact (raise from neon). Loom structure only; no community motifs copied.

STORY: The visitor sees Josh and his line, sees that America Voice is the headline work, opens it, understands how he designs and ships, and emails him or takes the résumé.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop 1440: the left ~40% holds the selvage column (name in display type, the one-line positioning, visible nav links Work · About · Email). Right: the portrait as a woven panel, warp threads crossing its edges, on the indigo field. The America Voice band starts above the fold at the bottom edge, full width, screens visible, labelled "Case study", and it is the primary action. Mobile: portrait panel, name and line, then the AV band within the first 1.3 screens.

FORM: Backstrap Loom, grounded candidate 7 of 7, seed:a9f513f2bb863b49. Signature interaction: beat-in, where a band's weft picks slide down and pack tight as it enters view, revealing screens; with reduced motion, bands render already beaten. Motion grammar: vertical packing only, one orchestration per band.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
